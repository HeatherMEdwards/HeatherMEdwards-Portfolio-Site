Heather M. Edwards

Writer/editor with extensive experience in multimedia storytelling, interviewing, and research for mission-driven organizations and online publications. Native English speaker, fluent Spanish speaker, learning French and Arabic.

Medium Substack

As a social critic and political essayist, Heather M. Edwards (she/her) writes news articles, feature pieces, human interest stories, interviews, website copy, newsletters, and blog posts for mission-driven organizations and publications. She is a co-founding member of Las Escritoras, a multi-genre writing, editing, and beta reader cooperative based in Guadalajara, Mexico. She also writes about empowering community and economic development projects and programs that Rotary International does locally, regionally, and globally.

Published in: The Good Men Project, Counter Arts, Fourth Wave, ProWritingAid, The Writing Cooperative, Writers Guild, Publishous, The Startup, The Ascent, The Political Prism, Interfaith Now, Equality Includes You
